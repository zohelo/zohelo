How to read

  • Inflation is the year-on-year change in prices: 2.9% means the basket costs 2.9% more than a year earlier — not that prices rose 2.9% in a month.
  • Falling inflation means prices rise more slowly, not that they fall. Changes in the rate are in percentage points (pp).
  • The Polish headline is Statistics Poland CPI; HICP (Eurostat) is for EU comparisons. The fixture has only HICP and World Bank CPI.
